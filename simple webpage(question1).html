MALLA REDDY ENGINEERING COLLEGE AND MANAGEMENT SCIENCES

Malla Reddy Engineering College and Management Sciences (MREM), located in Medchal, Telangana, is a distinguished institution affiliated with JNTU Hyderabad. Established in 2008, the college is set amidst a serene 10-acre campus, offering a pollution-free environment conducive to learning. MREM provides a range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering and management, including specialized courses in Computer Science (AI & ML, Cyber Security, IoT, Data Science), Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, and Electrical and Electronics Engineering. The college is equipped with modern facilities such as a placement cell, research and development center, sports amenities, and incubation centers to foster innovation and career growth. MREM also hosts engaging events like TECHSPHERE and alumni meets, creating a vibrant and interactive academic community. Admissions are open for 2025, with options for Convener Quota and Management Quota. You can explore more about the college on their official website.
